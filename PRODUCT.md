# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who know Finlay, encounter his work through Hourglass AI, or want a practical founder's view of AI. They are looking for useful writing, a sense of the person behind it, and a quick way to subscribe or keep reading.

## Product Purpose

Present Finlay and his work in a calm, personal space. The homepage should make his Friday newsletter the clearest next step while preserving the small details that make the site feel like him.

## Brand Personality

Calm, curious, and personal. The voice is concise and direct, with the quiet confidence of someone sharing what he is noticing and building rather than selling a product.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS landing pages, feature grids, invented social proof, competing cards, gradients, stock imagery, and inflated marketing claims. The site should not feel busy, corporate, or over-produced.

## Design Principles

- Make the useful next step obvious without making the page loud.
- Keep Finlay's interests and personal voice visible alongside his work.
- Prefer concrete language about real practice over broad claims.
- Reuse the site's established type, colour, spacing, and motion language.
- Give each viewport one clear centre of gravity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic structure, keyboard-accessible actions, visible focus treatment, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion behaviour. Maintain comfortable tap targets and avoid relying on colour alone.
